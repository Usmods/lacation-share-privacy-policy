Privacy Policy

App Name: System Update (Panda Tracker)

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to System Update ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your anti-theft recovery and location tracking security. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

To provide anti-theft recovery and real-time tracking functionality, the app collects the following data:

  • Location Data: Precise GPS and network location coordinates to track the device in case of loss or theft.
  • Device Information: Device manufacturer, model name, and secure Android ID to uniquely identify and manage your registered devices.
  • Device Status & Sensors: Battery level percentage and SIM card status (e.g., SIM removal detection) to trigger emergency security alerts.
  • User Provided Data: Owner or rider name entered during activation.

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used strictly for:

  • Real-time anti-theft recovery and location tracking on the authorized admin dashboard.
  • Sending instant push notifications and security alerts (such as SIM card removal or device online status).
  • Queuing location updates locally when offline and syncing them automatically upon internet restoration.

4. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted between the mobile application and our secure backend (Firebase Realtime Database) is encrypted in transit. Access to your tracking data is strictly restricted to the authorized admin dashboard.

5. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party cloud infrastructure services, specifically Google Firebase, for database storage, push notifications, and server-side processing.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
